## Introduction

The New Hampshire “Biden robocall” incident became one of the clearest early demonstrations of why election-related audio cannot be judged by ear alone. In January 2024, thousands of voters received automated calls that appeared to feature then-President Joe Biden's voice telling Democrats to stay home and “save” their votes for the November election rather than participate in the state's presidential primary. Investigators later concluded that the voice had been artificially generated and that the operation was part of a coordinated effort to influence voter behaviour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/f3469ceb6dd613079092287994663db5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The call, sent to multiple voters, falsely claimed that voting in the primary would support Republicans and urged voters to save their vo...</span></span></span>

## What the call was used to make voters believe

The robocall reached New Hampshire voters shortly before the Democratic primary. The message falsely suggested that participating in the primary would somehow help Republicans and that voters should instead wait until the general election. State officials characterised the calls as an apparent voter-suppression effort because the message attempted to discourage lawful participation in an election.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/f3469ceb6dd613079092287994663db5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News+2Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The call, sent to multiple voters, falsely claimed that voting in the primary would support Republicans and urged voters to save their vo...</span></span></span>

What made the case significant was not merely the political content. Election misinformation often succeeds by exploiting time pressure. Voters have only a limited window to verify information before polling day. A convincing voice that appears to come from a candidate or officeholder can create confusion at exactly the moment when clarification is hardest to obtain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2024/03/16/biden-deepfake-robocall-lawsuit-new-hampshire/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: washingtonpost.com">[The Washington Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">washingtonpost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">biden deepfake robocall lawsuit new hampshire</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Washington PostGroup sues after novel Biden deepfake urged voters to skip...Mar 17, 2024 — The January deepfake of Biden is one of t...</span></span></span>

The incident was widely viewed as one of the first major examples of generative AI being used in a US presidential-election context to discourage voting through a cloned political voice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2024/03/16/biden-deepfake-robocall-lawsuit-new-hampshire/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: washingtonpost.com">[The Washington Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">washingtonpost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">biden deepfake robocall lawsuit new hampshire</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Washington PostGroup sues after novel Biden deepfake urged voters to skip...Mar 17, 2024 — The January deepfake of Biden is one of t...</span></span></span>

## How officials and reporters traced the source trail

### The investigation did not depend on listening tests

A common misunderstanding is that investigators solved the case by detecting flaws in the synthetic voice. In reality, the key breakthroughs came from tracing the communication network that carried the calls.

New Hampshire authorities, working with federal agencies and industry traceback groups, followed telecom records to identify the companies involved in transmitting the robocalls. Investigators linked the operation to telecom firms including Life Corporation and Lingo Telecom, creating a documented path from the calls received by voters back through the communications infrastructure that delivered them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cyberscoop.com/lingo-life-biden-ai-robocall/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cyberscoop.com">[CyberScoop+2Forbes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cyberscoop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Hampshire authorities trace Biden AI robocall to...February 6, 2024 — 6 Feb 2024 — An investigation identified Life Corpor...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 6, 2024</span></span></span>

The caller ID itself provided another clue. Reports indicated that the operation spoofed the telephone number of a former New Hampshire Democratic Party chair, creating a false appearance of legitimacy while obscuring the true source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/f3469ceb6dd613079092287994663db5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The call, sent to multiple voters, falsely claimed that voting in the primary would support Republicans and urged voters to save their vo...</span></span></span>

### Following the people behind the operation

As the investigation progressed, authorities identified political consultant Steve Kramer as the organiser of the robocall campaign. Kramer later acknowledged orchestrating the calls. New Hampshire prosecutors brought criminal charges, while federal regulators pursued enforcement actions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/biden-robocalls-ai-new-hampshire-charges-fines-9e9cc63a71eb9c78b9bb0d1ec2aa6e9c" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News+2Business Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">biden robocalls ai new hampshire charges fines 9e9cc63a71eb9c78b9bb0d1ec2aa6e9c</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AP NewsPolitical consultant behind fake Biden robocalls faces $6...23 May 2024 — A former New Hampshire Democratic Party chair whose pho...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

The case illustrates a broader verification principle: determining who commissioned, created and distributed a piece of media is often more valuable than debating whether listeners can hear signs of manipulation. Once investigators reconstructed the chain of responsibility, the authenticity question became much easier to answer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/628/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: incidentdatabase.ai">[Incident Database]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">incidentdatabase.ai</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Incident DatabaseIncident 628: Fake Biden Voice in Robocall Misleads New...21 Jan 2024 — Description: A robocall imitating President Bid...</span></span></span>

### Telecom evidence mattered as much as the audio itself

The investigation eventually extended beyond the creator of the message. The US Federal Communications Commission pursued action against telecom companies involved in transmitting the calls. Lingo Telecom later agreed to pay a $1 million civil penalty and implement stricter compliance measures, while Kramer faced a proposed $6 million FCC penalty and separate state charges.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-404951A1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: docs.fcc.gov">[FCC Documentation+2Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">docs.fcc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FCC DocumentationLingo Telecom to Pay $1 Million Civil Penalty and...WASHINGTON, August 21, 2024—The Federal Communications Commission t...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

This aspect of the case is particularly instructive. The evidence did not rest on proving that every listener should have recognised an AI voice. Instead, regulators relied on records showing how the call travelled through telecommunications systems and who was responsible for initiating it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-404951A1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: docs.fcc.gov">[FCC Documentation+2FCC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">docs.fcc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FCC DocumentationLingo Telecom to Pay $1 Million Civil Penalty and...WASHINGTON, August 21, 2024—The Federal Communications Commission t...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2024</span></span></span>

## Election-season checks before sharing political audio

The New Hampshire case offers practical lessons for evaluating suspicious election audio.

**Ask where the recording first appeared.** If a dramatic political recording surfaces through anonymous accounts, mass messages or robocalls rather than identifiable campaign channels or recognised news organisations, treat it cautiously until independent confirmation emerges.

**Look for independent reporting, not reactions.** During the New Hampshire incident, state election officials, journalists and federal authorities quickly issued statements and began investigations. Verification came from those independent inquiries, not from social-media arguments about whether the voice “sounded real”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/f3469ceb6dd613079092287994663db5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News+2Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The call, sent to multiple voters, falsely claimed that voting in the primary would support Republicans and urged voters to save their vo...</span></span></span>

**Check whether election officials have responded.** When suspicious election audio appears close to voting day, local election authorities and attorneys general often publish warnings, [corrections]({{ 'corrections/' | relative_url }}) or investigative updates. ## Why the case changed thinking about election audio

The Biden robocall case demonstrated that modern election verification increasingly resembles investigative journalism and digital forensics rather than a contest of human perception. A cloned voice may sound convincing enough to fool many listeners. The stronger question is whether the message can be connected to a legitimate campaign, verified through independent sources and traced through a transparent chain of origin.

That distinction is central to credibility checks in the age of AI. The New Hampshire investigation succeeded because authorities reconstructed the evidence trail behind the recording. The lesson for voters is straightforward: when political audio appears at a strategically important moment, trust the traceable evidence surrounding the message more than the sound of the voice itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cyberscoop.com/lingo-life-biden-ai-robocall/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cyberscoop.com">[CyberScoop+2Incident Database]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cyberscoop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Hampshire authorities trace Biden AI robocall to...February 6, 2024 — 6 Feb 2024 — An investigation identified Life Corpor...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 6, 2024</span></span></span>
